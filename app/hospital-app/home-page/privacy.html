1. Introduction

Our hospital is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Hospital Management System website and services.

We may collect information about you in various ways:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, access patterns

3. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our healthcare services

4. Protection of Your Information

We implement a variety of security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL encryption for all data transmissions
  • Secure password authentication
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to sensitive information
  • Compliance with HIPAA regulations

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties except as required by law or with your explicit consent.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your information
