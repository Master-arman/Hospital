Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I register for an online account?

A: Click on the "New Patient" button on the home page, fill in your personal details, and create a secure password. Your account will be activated immediately.

Q: Can I book an appointment online?

A: Yes! Log in to your account, navigate to the appointments section, select your preferred department and doctor, choose your time slot, and confirm your booking.

Q: How do I access my medical records?

A: Login to your patient portal. All your medical records, test results, and prescriptions will be available in the "My Records" section.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: We accept credit cards, debit cards, online banking transfers, and digital wallets. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Q: Is my patient data secure?

A: Yes, we follow HIPAA compliance standards and use advanced encryption to protect all patient information. Your privacy is our priority.

Q: How can I reschedule an appointment?

A: Go to "My Appointments" in your dashboard, select the appointment you want to reschedule, and choose a new time slot from available options.

Q: Do you offer emergency services?

A: Yes, our emergency department is open 24/7. In case of emergency, call our ambulance service immediately or visit our nearest facility.
