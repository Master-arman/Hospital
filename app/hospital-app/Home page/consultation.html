💻 Online Consultation Services

  • Follow-up: Receive digital prescription and follow-up instructions

  • ⏱️ Save Time: No travel required, consult from anywhere

  • 🔒 Privacy: Secure and confidential consultation environment
  • 🏥 Quality Care: Same professionals, convenient format
  • 📱 Easy Access: Works on all devices - mobile, tablet, or computer
